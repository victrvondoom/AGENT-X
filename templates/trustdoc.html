TrustDoc

A regulated document, turned into a signed and compliant one — where every field is confidence-routed, anything uncertain is held for a human, and the result is verifiable by someone who does not trust this application.

1 · Upload

The document goes to Nutrient DWS /extract, which returns each field with a confidence signal and a citation back to the page region.

2 · Confidence gate

Per-field-type thresholds, not one number. Absent confidence is its own case — it means no signal, not low, so it always goes to a person.

3 · Human review

Nothing downstream runs until every held field has been ruled on. The guard is in SQL, so it cannot be bypassed by calling the API differently.

4 · Generate, sign, self-verify

After signing, the agent re-reads the document it just produced and compares every field to what the human approved. A mismatch fails the job.

5 · Verify it yourself

Four independent ways to check this without trusting us.
