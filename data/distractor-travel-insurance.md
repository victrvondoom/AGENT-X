# Annual Multi-Trip Travel Insurance — Summary

**Insured:** Alex Tan
**Area of cover:** Europe
**Period:** 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31
**Trip limit:** 31 days per trip

## Benefits
1. Emergency medical expenses up to £5,000,000.
2. Cancellation and curtailment up to £3,000.
3. Baggage up to £1,500 (single-article limit £250).

## Conditions and exclusions
- Pre-existing medical conditions must be declared.
- Winter sports excluded unless the winter-sports extension is purchased.
- Valuables left unattended in a public place are not covered.
- Claims must be notified within 28 days of return.
