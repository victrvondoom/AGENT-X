# Home Contents Insurance Policy — Schedule & Key Terms

**Policyholder:** Alex Tan
**Insured address:** Flat 4B, 12 Maple Court, Bristol
**Policy type:** Contents-only (tenant). Buildings are NOT insured by this policy.
**Sum insured (total contents):** £25,000
**Policy period:** 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31

## What is covered
1. **Theft of personal belongings from the insured home**, including by forced
   entry, is covered up to the sum insured.
2. Electronic items (laptops, phones, tablets) are covered as personal belongings,
   **subject to the portable-electronics endorsement** — see condition below.
3. Accidental damage to contents is covered as standard.

## Limits and conditions
- **General single-item limit:** any one item worth more than **£1,500** is only
  covered if it has been **individually scheduled**. Specific limits and conditions
  for certain categories are varied by endorsement.
- **Portable electronics:** laptops, tablets and cameras are governed by the
  **Portable Electronic Equipment endorsement (E-12)** recorded in your
  **Schedule of Endorsements** — refer to that document for the applicable limit
  and conditions. This policy schedule does not restate those terms.
- **Police reporting:** all theft must be **reported to the police within 48 hours**
  and a **crime reference number** obtained, or the claim is invalid.

## Items specified on this schedule
- High-value articles are listed in the Schedule of Endorsements, not here.
