# Schedule of Endorsements & Specified Valuables — Ref MC-4B/2026

This schedule forms part of the contents cover and records the agreed endorsements and any
individually scheduled articles. Endorsements amend the base wording; where an endorsement
applies, its terms override the general conditions.

## Endorsement register
- **E-03 — Accidental Damage Extension.** Active. Standard excess £75 per claim.
- **E-07 — Escape of Water.** Active. Trace-and-access limited to £5,000.
- **E-12 — Portable Electronic Equipment.** Active. See terms below.
- **E-19 — Cycle Cover.** Inactive (no premium paid).

## E-12 terms (Portable Electronic Equipment)
Portable electronic equipment — laptops, tablets, cameras and handheld games consoles —
attracts the following amendments to the base contents wording:
1. Each such item is settled to a maximum of **£1,500** unless individually scheduled in the
   "Specified articles" table below at an agreed value.
2. Cover for these items applies **only where the property's fitted window locks were engaged**
   at the time of loss; where they were not, claims for these items are declined.
3. These items are **not covered while temporarily removed from the insured address** unless
   individually scheduled with the "away-from-home" flag set.
