# Private Car Insurance — Certificate & Key Terms

**Policyholder:** Alex Tan
**Vehicle:** Volkswagen Polo, reg MC26 TAN
**Cover type:** Comprehensive
**Period:** 2026-03-01 to 2027-02-28
**Annual premium:** £612

## Cover
1. Accidental damage, fire and theft of the vehicle.
2. Third-party liability up to £20,000,000.
3. Windscreen cover with £25 excess.

## Conditions
- Voluntary excess £250; compulsory excess £150.
- Named drivers only. No cover for business use beyond commuting.
- Vehicle must have a valid MOT. Keys must not be left in the vehicle.

## No-claims discount
4 years protected. One at-fault claim permitted before the discount steps back.
