## What this warranty covers
1. Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.
2. Hardware component failure under normal use (battery, mainboard, display panel).
3. Free repair or like-for-like replacement at an authorised service centre.

## What this warranty does NOT cover
This warranty covers defects only. It explicitly does **not** cover:
- **Theft, loss, or disappearance** of the device.
- Accidental damage (drops, liquid spills, cracked screens).
- Cosmetic wear or damage caused by misuse.

## Optional add-on (NOT purchased)
An "AeroCare+ Theft & Accidental Damage" plan was offered at purchase for an
extra fee. The owner **declined** this add-on. Without AeroCare+, theft and
accidental damage are not covered under any manufacturer plan.

## Making a defect claim
Contact the authorised service centre with proof of purchase. Theft claims are
not accepted and should be directed to the owner's own insurer.
