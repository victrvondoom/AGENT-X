# Assured Shorthold Tenancy Agreement

**Landlord:** Maple Court Lettings Ltd
**Tenant:** Alex Tan
**Property:** Flat 4B, 12 Maple Court, Bristol
**Term:** 12 months from 2026-01-01

## Insurance responsibilities (Clause 7)
1. The **landlord insures the building structure only**. The landlord's policy
   does **not** cover the tenant's personal possessions or contents.
2. The **tenant is responsible for insuring their own contents and belongings**.
   The landlord strongly recommends the tenant hold a contents insurance policy.

## Security obligations (Clause 9)
1. The tenant must keep the property **secure**: lock all external doors and
   **engage the fitted window locks** whenever the flat is left unattended.
2. Failure to secure the property may **invalidate the tenant's own insurance
   claims** and may make the tenant liable for resulting loss.

## Reporting (Clause 12)
1. Any break-in, theft, or damage must be reported to the landlord in writing
   **within 24 hours** of discovery.
2. The tenant must also report any crime to the police directly.

## Alterations (Clause 14)
The tenant may not change locks or alter window fittings without written consent.
